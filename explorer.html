Definitions

  • Year — fall term / start of academic year (2023 = fall 2023 / 2023-24). Completions use the matching academic year.
  • 12-month FTE — IPEDS EFIA undergraduate + graduate + doctor’s-professional FTE for the academic year.
  • Age of plant — accumulated depreciation ÷ annual depreciation expense (IPEDS F1A, FY 2022-23). A common proxy for average facility age; >20 years usually signals reinvestment need. Values outside 1–60 years (partial or parent-level reporting) are suppressed.
  • O&M $ / FTE — operations & maintenance of plant expense ÷ 12-month FTE.
  • % fully online — share of fall students enrolled exclusively in distance education (DRVEF).
  • Campuses that report finance through a district or system parent show “—” for finance-based facility metrics.
  • Campus-page indicators — enrollment change ≤ −10% / ≥ +10% over 5 yrs; ≥ 25% fully online; 12-mo FTE < 60% of headcount (mostly part-time); age of plant ≥ 20 yrs (older) or ≤ 12 yrs (newer); O&M per FTE ≥ 25% above or ≤ 25% below the system median; construction in progress > $1M.
